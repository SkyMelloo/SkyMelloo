# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

SkyMelloo's community - GitHub Issues and Discussions, the sky.melloo.me website (friends, relay
chat, status messages, comments), and the mod's own in-game features - is open to everyone. Nobody
is excluded, harassed, or treated differently because of who they are: their race, ethnicity,
nationality, gender identity or expression, sexual orientation, disability, age, religion, or any
other personal characteristic. This applies regardless of experience level, how long you've been
around, or whether you're a contributor, a player, or just passing through.

Respectful, friendly conduct is the baseline expectation everywhere in this project - not an
aspiration.

## Not okay

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory remarks directed at anyone, in any SkyMelloo space.
- Deliberately excluding, mocking, or piling on someone because of who they are.
- Sharing someone else's private information without their consent.
- Sustained disruption of Issues, Discussions, chat, or any other shared space.

## Enforcement

There is zero tolerance for the behavior above. A confirmed violation results in an immediate
restriction - this can mean losing access to GitHub Issues/Discussions, a website account
suspension, an in-game block, or any combination, at the maintainers' discretion. There's no
escalation ladder required before that happens; a single serious incident is enough.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness behavior like this, please report it rather than letting it slide:

- Email **maja@melloo.me**
- Discord: **HexedMaya**
- On the website, use the Report option next to a friend's status message, or the
  [Report a Bug](https://sky.melloo.me/report-bug) form for anything else that needs the team's
  attention.

Reports are handled by the project's admins and moderators and kept confidential from the person
being reported.
